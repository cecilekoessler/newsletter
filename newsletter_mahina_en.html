Dear residents, sea users, and local partners, 'Ia ora na,

On the occasion of the official opening of the humpback whale watching season (July 20 to November 20, 2025) in our municipality's waters and off Matavai Bay, the City of Mahina actively joins the country's efforts by promoting the awareness campaign 'A pāruru tō mau parāoa, encouraging both locals and visitors to adopt respectful behavior toward these magnificent creatures.

Our coastline, beaches, and scenic spots make Mahina a privileged natural observatory for these gentle ocean giants. In this municipal newsletter, find all the essential safety rules, the updated maritime regulations, coexistence best practices, and local initiatives to observe whales without disturbing their peace.

📏 Maritime Regulations in Municipal Waters

Dolphins and humpback whales are strictly protected species. Municipal police and sworn environmental officers monitor adherence to maritime safety standards:

Mandatory Minimum Distances
• 100 meters (330 ft) for professional guides and operators holding official DIREN certification.
• 300 meters (985 ft) for all recreational boaters, coastal fishers, and non-motorized craft (va'a, jet-skis, stand-up paddles).
Navigation & Precautions
• Speed strictly limited to 3 knots within the 300-meter radius.
• Maintain a trajectory strictly parallel to the cetaceans.
• Strictly prohibited to cross their path, chase them, or separate a mother from her calf.
👥 Choose Certified Professional Operators

Planning to share this spectacle with family or book a sea tour? The City of Mahina urges residents and visitors to book exclusively with licensed operators listed on the official PARAOA platform run by the Direction of the Environment (DIREN).

📸 Drone Flights & Coastal Filming

Pointe Vénus and our shoreline are popular spots for photography and filming. Please be reminded that flying drones directly over marine mammals at low altitudes is strictly forbidden without a special permit due to acoustic disturbance. Commercial use of footage of protected species requires prior authorization from DIREN.

🌊 Land-Based Whale Watching: The Hono Tai Initiative

In partnership with the Oceania association, the City of Mahina champions land-based whale watching. From our cliffs, lookouts, and coastal heights overlooking Matavai Bay, enjoy whale breaches and blows with a simple pair of binoculars—free of engine noise and completely harmless to marine life.
